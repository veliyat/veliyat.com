Problems I solved

From a client's problem to a working solution

  • Optivo Health

    Keeping eye contact with the patient

    Problem

    Clinicians had to keep typing during the exam instead of looking at their patient.

    Solution

    Speech-to-text dictation that turns the clinician's spoken notes into structured exam data and clinical notes.

    • Google Speech-to-Text
    • Gemini on Vertex AI
  • Agile Infoways · client project

    Publishing to many screens at once

    Problem

    A client needed one publishing service that could put content on many screens at the same time.

    Solution

    I proposed using browsers as the screens, with Socket.IO pushing content to them in real time: PDFs, slides, images and video, plus annotations drawn from a phone.

    • Socket.IO
    • Browsers as displays
  • LeGuard · Agile Infoways

    Live visibility without live sockets

    Problem

    Legal departments needed an up-to-date view of outside counsel's work and fees.

    Solution

    Before WebSockets were common, we kept the views current by polling the server with jQuery and Ajax. I led the project and its architecture decisions.

    • jQuery
    • Ajax polling
    • CakePHP
